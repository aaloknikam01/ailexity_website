Company Profile

One studio, two engines: our own products and your systems.

Ailexity does two things with full focus. We build and operate our own software products — Ailexity POS and Ailexity Tedo. And we design the systems companies run on: software systems, operational systems, and the sales and marketing funnels that keep them fed with customers.

Because we run products of our own, we design client systems like owners rather than vendors — clear scopes, practical builds, and systems that keep working long after handover. Based in Pune, working locally and remotely.

How It Works

From Idea to Digital Reality

A clear, proven process that takes your vision from whiteboard to world-class product.

Step 01 Week 0–1
Discovery

We map how your business runs today — goals, bottlenecks, current tools — and define exactly what the system needs to do.

You get
  • Scope document & fixed quote
  • System map of your operations
  • Delivery timeline
Step 02 Week 1–2
Design

We design the system and the funnel around it — interfaces, workflows, and the path that turns a visitor into a customer.

You get
  • Wireframes & UI designs
  • Funnel & workflow blueprint
  • Build plan you approve
Step 03 Week 2–5
Build

Engineering, integrations, and content come together — with weekly check-ins so you see progress, not surprises.

You get
  • Working system on staging
  • Integrations & automations
  • Testing & fixes before launch
Step 04 Ongoing
Launch & Grow

We deploy, train your team, and stay on — monitoring, maintenance, and funnel tuning as your numbers come in.

You get
  • Go-live & team training
  • Analytics & reporting setup
  • Support & growth plan

Typical engagement: 2–6 weeks from first call to launch, depending on scope. Products like websites and funnels ship faster; full systems take longer.
